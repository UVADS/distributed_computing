    Try this

    1. Click TERM-0 three times. Every record lands in partition 1, and its offsets keep counting up. Notebook §3
    2. Click a key in the legend. All of that key's records sit in one partition, in the order they were sent. Records in different partitions have no order relative to each other. §5
    3. Add consumers to fraud-detector until there are four. With three partitions, the fourth has nothing to read. §7
    4. Poll analytics, then reset it to earliest. Its lag jumps back to the whole log, and the next poll replays everything. §8
    5. Reset analytics to earliest, click Apply retention twice, then Poll analytics. Its committed offsets point at deleted data, so the poll fails with OffsetOutOfRange until you reset it. §8
    6. Turn on the live stream and auto-poll for fraud-detector only. One group keeps up while the other falls further behind. §6–7

    Broker

    Retention deletes the oldest closed segment in each partition. The active segment, the one still being written, is never deleted. Real Kafka deletes by age (retention.ms) or size (retention.bytes).

    Uses the same partitioner as kafka_with_pyspark_demo.ipynb, crc32 of the key mod 3, so TERM-0 lands in partition 1 here and in the notebook. Real Kafka uses murmur2. Not simulated: replication, leader election, and the group coordinator.
